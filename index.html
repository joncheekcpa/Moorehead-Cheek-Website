Questions

Frequently asked.

How do I get started?

Reach out using the form below or give us a call. We'll discuss your needs, then send a written proposal with a flat fee. Once approved, you'll receive a secure portal invitation and a personalized document checklist.

How is your pricing structured?

Most engagements are quoted as a flat fee based on complexity, so there are no hourly surprises. Monthly accounting is offered as a fixed monthly subscription.

Can you help if I'm behind on filings or received an IRS notice?

Yes. We regularly help clients catch up on prior-year returns and respond to IRS and state notices. The sooner you reach out, the more options there typically are.

Do I need to meet in person?

Not at all. We work with clients in person, by video and entirely online. Documents are exchanged through our encrypted client portal, and returns can be signed electronically.

Which accounting software do you support?

We work primarily in QuickBooks Online and can help you set up, clean up or migrate your books. Ask us about other platforms.
