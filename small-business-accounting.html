Questions

Common questions.

Do I need to use QuickBooks Online?

We work primarily in QuickBooks Online and can help you set up, clean up or migrate your books. Ask us about other platforms.

How is monthly accounting priced?

Monthly accounting is offered as a fixed monthly subscription based on the size and complexity of your business, so there are no hourly surprises.

Can you start in the middle of the year?

Yes. We can start at any point and catch up earlier months as needed.

Will you work with my tax return too?

Yes. When we keep your books and prepare your return, everything is coordinated, which saves time and reduces errors.
