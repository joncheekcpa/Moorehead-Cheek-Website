Questions

Common questions.

When should I send my documents?

As soon as you have them. Getting documents to us early gives more time for review and planning, and helps avoid a last-minute rush.

What if I can't get everything by the deadline?

We can file an extension when needed. Keep in mind that an extension gives you more time to file, not more time to pay, so we'll help you estimate any balance due.

I received a letter from the IRS. What should I do?

Don't ignore it, and don't panic. Send us a copy through the secure portal and we'll explain what it means and how to respond.

Do you prepare returns for other states?

Yes. We prepare multi-state returns for individuals and businesses with income or operations in more than one state.
